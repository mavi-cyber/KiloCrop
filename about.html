Your photo never leaves your device.

KiloCrop crops photos to exact shapes, passport and visa standards, common aspect ratios, or any shape you like and saves them at the pixel size and file size an application form asks for.

Private by design

Everything happens inside your browser. When you open a photo it is read locally; cropping, rotating, resizing and saving all run on your phone or computer. There is no server that receives images, no account and no tracking.

Saving creates a brand-new image file, so hidden details stored in the original - such as GPS location and camera information - are not carried over.

Works offline

After your first visit KiloCrop keeps working without an internet connection. Install it from your browser’s menu (“Add to Home Screen” or “Install app”) to use it like a native app.

Check the official rules

Photo requirements change from time to time. The document presets are a starting point - always confirm the current size, background and framing rules with the authority you are applying to. The head guide is an alignment aid, not an approval.
