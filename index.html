Location and camera details

When a phone or camera takes a photo, it quietly saves extra information inside the file. You can't see it in the picture, but anyone who gets the file can read it with free tools.

Location (GPS)

The exact spot where the photo was taken, often within a few metres. For photos taken at home it can reveal your address.

Camera details

Phone or camera model, lens, settings like exposure and ISO, the date and time it was taken, and the editing app.

Should I keep them?

  • Remove both Passport, visa, ID, exam or job forms. Offices only need the picture, and extra data just adds size.
  • Remove location Posting online or sending to people you don't know. Location can show where you live, work or study.
  • Keep camera only Photography portfolios or contests. Shows your gear and settings without giving away places.
  • Keep both Your own backups and albums. Photo apps use date and place to sort and map your memories.

Not sure? Leave both off - that's the safe choice. KiloCrop always removes camera serial numbers and the hidden thumbnail (which can show the uncropped photo). Your original file is never changed.
